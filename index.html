affiliate-charts

Funnels, money waterfalls, daily series, KPI tiles and rankings for partner cabinets, drawn as SVG strings. Every control below is an option of one call.

The classic shape draws one positive series: counts only. Money funnels have two bars a step, losses and negatives.

An area draws one series: two would hide each other. This example has two, so it is drawn as lines.

The shape leaves out part steps such as unique clicks: they are subsets, not stages.
